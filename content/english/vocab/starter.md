单词卡片用 `::::word` 写,和知识卡片(`::::card`)是**两条独立轨道**:它们不进 `/复习`,而在 [/english](/english/) 的「单词」页用间隔重复背诵、拼写测验。一个单词一个块。

::::word{ipa="/əˈbændən/" pos=v}
abandon

:::meaning
放弃,抛弃;遗弃(某人/某地)
:::

:::example
He abandoned his car in the snow and walked home.
:::
::::

::::word{ipa="/ˈkændɪd/" pos=adj}
candid

:::meaning
坦率的,直言的;(照片)抓拍的
:::

:::example
She gave a candid account of what went wrong.
:::
::::

::::word{ipa="/dɪˈlɪbərət/" pos=adj}
deliberate

:::meaning
故意的,蓄意的;从容不迫的
:::

:::example
His silence was deliberate — he wanted them to worry.
:::
::::

::::word{ipa="/ˌɛləˈkwɛnt/" pos=adj}
eloquent

:::meaning
雄辩的,有说服力的;(表情/动作)传神的
:::

:::example
Her eloquent speech moved the whole room to tears.
:::
::::

::::word{ipa="/prɪˈveɪl/" pos=v}
prevail

:::meaning
盛行,普遍存在;(最终)获胜,占上风
:::

:::example
Common sense prevailed and the plan was dropped.
:::
::::
